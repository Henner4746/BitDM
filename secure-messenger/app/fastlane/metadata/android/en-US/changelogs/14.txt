Fixes 19 bugs found in a two-device test, including:
* A read receipt could mark older, still unsent messages (scheduled or stuck offline) as read, so they were never sent
* Groups were missing from the chat list after a restart
* Spoilers leaked into previews, quotes and search
* Dialogs, sheets and date pickers now match the app style; no more colour emojis as controls
* Read receipts are on by default, as the settings already claimed
