BitDM now encrypts with Signal's official libsignal library. New sessions are protected against future quantum computers with Kyber-1024. Not compatible with 1.9.x: both sides have to update. Until then your messages wait, and the chat says why. Devices can be signed out; adding another device needs a code from one you already have. Attachments now work on Windows and Linux too. New input row: everything the same height, and Send fills in as soon as you type.
